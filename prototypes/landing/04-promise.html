QUESTIONS STUDENTS ASK

Where do the quiz questions come from?
From paragraphs of the slides you just read, and every question cites its paragraph so you can check it was not invented. Questions that fail that check are dropped, and nothing is made up to fill the gap.
What does the readiness number mean?
The mean of what your quizzes have weighed, on the topics you have sat. It is a reading of your record, not a prediction of your exam, and it refuses to appear until enough topics have a sitting to be measured.
What happens when I miss a study day?
The plan moves what is waiting to your other days. Missed days shift, they are never squeezed in, and the week does not scold you for it.
What does it cost?
Conversion, reading and model calls are metered, and the rates are shown before you spend. One deliberate cost to know: re-uploading a file is re-paying for its conversion.
