How it works

Four steps, in the order you would actually do them. Nothing here is a demo mode: what you see after signing up is this flow on your own course.

1

Upload your course PDFs

Decks from different weeks are fine. Reading is metered: a file is converted once and you pay for it once, so re-uploading is re-paying. A file that cannot be read says so and leaves the others alone.

2

Check the topic path

Mastory infers the topics your material covers and points each one at its slides. You correct it before anything is scheduled on it: rename topics, set how long each takes, take off what is not on the exam.

3

Get your week

The plan fits inside the time you say you have, on the days you say you have it. Miss a day and the plan shifts. It never squeezes what is waiting into a day that is already full.

4

Read, quiz, sit the mock exam

Reading opens at the slides the topic points at. Each topic's quiz is five questions from those paragraphs, marked instantly. The mock exam is one attempt, and it ends in a readiness report: a reading of your quizzes, not a prediction of your exam.

A week you can keep to

No streaks, no badges, no guilt. Your answers and your record stay yours: the readiness report reads them off on the day you open it, and nothing else is kept.
