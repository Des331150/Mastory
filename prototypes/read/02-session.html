Factors controlling the rate

Slides 71–76 of your weeks 1–6 deck · open the original slide 71

The rate depends on energy supply and on how easily vapour is carried away from the surface. The deck lists six factors; four are examined most often:

  • Temperature: more energy, more vapour.
  • Wind: carries vapour away from the surface.
  • Humidity of the air above: the closer to saturation, the slower.
  • Area and depth of the water body: bigger and shallower evaporates faster.

The remaining two, solar radiation and air pressure, appear in the derivation questions rather than the define-and-explain ones.

Worth knowing: the 2021 paper asked "explain three factors", any three of these four were accepted, which is why the deck keeps them in one list.
