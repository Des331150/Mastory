Mock exam: Basic Hydrology

One exam, one attempt

This is the whole course in one paper: questions written from your own slides, up to 3 of them for every topic, in the order the course teaches them. Any question that cannot be checked against the paragraph it came from is left out rather than shown.

You get one attempt. The questions are written once and are not written again, nothing is spent on a sitting, and the marks, the score and the paragraph behind every wrong answer all come back together when you hand the paper in, not before.

Nothing has been written yet. A paper is written when you ask for one, not when you open this page.

Topic quizzes are not spent by this and nothing is taken off them: sit those as often as you like, before or after.
